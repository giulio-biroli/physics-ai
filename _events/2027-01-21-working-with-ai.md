A series of short, informal presentations by students, PhD candidates and researchers showing how they use AI tools in their day-to-day research: calculations, coding, retrieving data, literature search and writing.

The presentations are followed by a discussion of good practice: how to check the output, how to keep track of what was done, and which uses are acceptable in papers and theses.
