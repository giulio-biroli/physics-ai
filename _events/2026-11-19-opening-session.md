The opening session gives an overview of the changes under way. Which parts of research in physics are already being transformed by AI — data analysis, simulation, symbolic calculation, literature search, the writing of papers and code — and which are likely to follow? What are the consequences for publishing, hiring and the training of students?

The talk will be followed by an open discussion.
