Why this seminar

AI heralds a profound change in every scientific discipline. Mathematics is already experiencing it head-on, and physics is beginning to as well.

Now is the right time to reflect together on what this means for the way we do physics — from theory to experiment — and for publishing, hiring and training; and on how to use AI well, keeping our role as researchers at the centre. These questions matter for researchers, and even more for students, PhD candidates and early-career researchers.
