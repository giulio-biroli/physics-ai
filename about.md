## Aims

AI heralds a profound change in every scientific discipline. Mathematics is already experiencing it head-on, and physics is now beginning to as well.

This is the right time to reflect together on how AI will transform the way we do physics — from theory to experiment — as well as publishing, hiring and training. These questions are many and pressing. It is important to address them as well as we can: for researchers, and even more so for students, PhD candidates and early-career researchers. A central theme is the good use of AI: using it in a way that keeps our role as researchers at the centre, and continues to maintain and develop it.

The seminar features talks on several aspects of this new era, in particular:

- **Perspectives** — overviews of how AI could transform the discipline, its practices and education;
- **Results** — the physics content of new results obtained with AI, and how AI was used to obtain them;
- **Methods** — how to use AI well in physics, for researchers, PhD candidates and students.

## Format

Sessions can take different formats: a talk followed by an open discussion, a round table, a series of informal presentations, and others. They take place at the Laboratoire de Physique of the École normale supérieure – PSL in Paris (24 rue Lhomond, 75005 Paris), in room Conf. IV, and are open to everyone; students, PhD candidates and early-career researchers are particularly welcome. Slides and, when possible, recordings are made available under [Resources]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources/).
